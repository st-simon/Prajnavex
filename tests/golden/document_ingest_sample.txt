A reliable knowledge pipeline separates extraction from approval. Automated
systems may propose a source summary and candidate knowledge cards, but a human
reviewer must verify the claims against the original evidence before promotion.

Each knowledge card should express one reusable idea. Cards that combine
several unrelated claims are difficult to retrieve, validate, and update.

Before creating a new card, the pipeline should compare its meaning with
existing cards. Similarity is advisory: the reviewer decides whether the new
card is a duplicate, an extension, or merely related context.
