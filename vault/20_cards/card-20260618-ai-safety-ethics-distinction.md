# 区分 AI 安全与 AI 伦理可以扩大治理共识面

## Core Idea

AI ethics asks what values machines and societies should embody. AI safety asks how to prevent systems from causing serious harm. The article argues that safety is often easier to operationalize across ideological and civilizational divides because it can be expressed through risks, tests, standards, audits, and response procedures.

## When Useful

Use this card when designing or evaluating international AI cooperation. If ethical consensus is blocked, safety cooperation may still proceed through shared risk language and technical standards.

## How To Apply

Separate the agenda into two tracks:

- Safety track: risk identification, red lines, evaluation, incident response, robustness, misuse prevention.
- Ethics track: fairness, dignity, autonomy, cultural values, distributive justice, human flourishing.

Then ask which items can become near-term common standards and which require longer procedural dialogue.
