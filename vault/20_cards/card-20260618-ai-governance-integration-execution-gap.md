# AI 治理首先面对整合力与执行力两道门槛

## Core Idea

AI governance faces two structural bottlenecks. The first is integration: governments, firms, legislatures, international organizations, and civil society hold different priorities about innovation, safety, sovereignty, and rights. The second is execution: many AI principles, declarations, and standards lack legal force or institutional machinery.

## When Useful

Use this card when evaluating whether an AI governance proposal is more than a principle statement. A serious proposal should show how competing norm systems are coordinated and how rules are implemented, audited, enforced, or revised.

## How To Apply

Ask two questions:

- What conflicts among norm systems does this proposal actually integrate?
- What mechanism makes the norm operational after agreement?

If either answer is missing, the proposal is probably symbolic, not yet institutional.
