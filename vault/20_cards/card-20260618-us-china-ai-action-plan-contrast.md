# 中美 AI 行动计划都越过纯伦理本位，但治理想象不同

## Core Idea

The article contrasts two AI policy imaginaries. The US approach is framed as winning AI leadership through innovation, infrastructure, standards, export controls, and strategic advantage. The Chinese approach is framed as AI as an international public good, with sovereignty, multilateral cooperation, open platforms, capacity-building, and shared technical standards.

## When Useful

Use this card when comparing AI governance documents that appear to use similar tools but serve different geopolitical models.

## How To Apply

Separate the instrument from the order it supports:

- Instrument: testing, evaluation, sandboxes, safety systems, standards, infrastructure.
- Order model: hierarchy and leadership, or multilateral cooperation and public-good provision.

Two countries can use similar technical-procedural instruments while pursuing different international orders.
