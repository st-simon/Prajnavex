# 正当程序可以成为 AI 价值与规范的生成模型

## Core Idea

The article treats procedure as more than an administrative safeguard. In complex AI systems, fair procedure can generate norms by structuring how humans, institutions, and machines justify decisions to one another. This shifts alignment from fixed value insertion toward ongoing reason-giving and contestable interaction.

## When Useful

Use this card when thinking about human-machine dialogue, explainable AI, AI mediation, AI assistants, or model governance. It is especially useful when "value alignment" seems too abstract or culturally contested.

## How To Apply

Design alignment around procedural conditions:

- The system must give reasons that affected users can understand.
- Users must have a channel to challenge or correct outputs.
- Risk decisions must be documented and reviewable.
- Conflicting values must be surfaced rather than hidden inside code.
- Dialogue should be repeatable enough to create institutional trust.

This makes procedure a practical route to legitimacy under uncertainty.
