# 伦理本位 AI 治理的弱点在于价值难定义、难对齐、难执行

## Core Idea

Ethics-centered AI governance has a real role, but it becomes fragile when treated as the primary global foundation. Different societies disagree about moral priority, and abstract ethical language is hard to convert into operational standards, technical tests, legal duties, and cross-border enforcement.

## When Useful

Use this card when a governance proposal relies heavily on broad terms such as "human-centered", "AI for good", "fairness", or "trustworthy AI" without specifying procedures, tests, institutions, or accountability.

## How To Apply

Translate the ethical claim into implementation checks:

- Which value is being protected?
- Who defines the value?
- What happens when values conflict?
- What technical or procedural evidence proves compliance?
- Who has authority to challenge the result?

If these questions cannot be answered, the ethical principle is not yet a governance mechanism.
