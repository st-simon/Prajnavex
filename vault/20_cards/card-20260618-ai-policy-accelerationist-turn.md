# 2025 年后 AI 政策钟摆转向科技加速主义

## Core Idea

The article treats technology accelerationism as a policy climate, not only a philosophical school. In this climate, states compete to accelerate AI development, reduce regulatory friction, and build national or corporate advantage. Safety remains present, but it is increasingly reframed as something that must support, not slow, innovation.

## When Useful

Use this card when interpreting why AI regulation appears to soften, delay, or become more standards-based during periods of strategic competition.

## How To Apply

When reading a new AI policy, check whether its default posture is restraint or acceleration:

- Does it remove regulatory barriers?
- Does it emphasize national competitiveness?
- Does it move safety into testing, evaluation, sandboxing, and technical assurance?
- Does it frame AI infrastructure as strategic industrial policy?

If yes, the policy likely belongs to the accelerationist turn.
