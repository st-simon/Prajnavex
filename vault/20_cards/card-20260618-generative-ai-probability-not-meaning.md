# 生成式 AI 通往概率世界，不自动产生意义世界

## Core Idea

The article links generative AI to the Polanyi problem: humans know more than they can explicitly state. Large models become powerful through prediction, scale, in-context learning, and meta-learning, but prediction is not the same as meaning. This is why AI may become more capable without becoming a source of humanistic value.

## When Useful

Use this card when analyzing claims that scaling AI will automatically solve ethical, cultural, or political meaning problems.

## How To Apply

Keep three layers separate:

- Capability: better prediction, generation, generalization, and tool use.
- Control: safety, robustness, monitoring, and procedural accountability.
- Meaning: human judgment, social values, cultural interpretation, and ethical innovation.

AI can support the first two layers, but the third remains a human responsibility.
