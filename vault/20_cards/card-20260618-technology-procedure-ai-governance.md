# 技术-程序本位把 AI 治理从价值宣言转向可验证过程

## Core Idea

The article's central move is to treat technical governance and procedural justice as a joint paradigm. Technical systems can test, monitor, evaluate, and constrain AI risks. Procedures can make those controls explainable, contestable, fair, and socially legitimate. Together they form a bridge between machine safety and human values.

## When Useful

Use this card when a policy debate is stuck between "regulate AI by ethics" and "let technology develop freely". The technology-procedure frame creates a third path: accelerate technical capability while requiring structured safety, verification, accountability, and dialogue.

## How To Apply

Evaluate whether the governance design contains both halves:

- Technology: benchmarks, audits, model evaluations, monitoring, safety tooling, secure deployment, interoperability standards.
- Procedure: notice, explanation, contestability, risk communication, stakeholder dialogue, independent review, proportional enforcement.

If only technology exists, governance risks becoming opaque technocracy. If only procedure exists, governance risks becoming slow symbolic compliance.
